Return Policy

Easy returns within 7 days

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy, we're here to help.

1. Return Eligibility

Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered.

2. Non-Returnable Items

Certain items such as perishables, personal care products, and clearance items cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons.

3. How to Return

Contact our support team with your order number and reason for return. We'll guide you through the process.

4. Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds will be processed within 5–7 working days via the original payment method.

5. Exchange Policy

If you'd prefer an exchange instead of a refund, we'll happily replace the item subject to availability.

6. Shipping Costs

Return shipping is free for damaged or incorrect items. For other returns, shipping costs may apply.

7. Contact for Returns

Reach out at support@mateenstore.com or call +92 300 1234567 to initiate a return.
